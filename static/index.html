            How it works

            The checker looks at a document in two ways.

            1. Instant check

            Runs in your browser and scores eight signals that often show up in AI-generated English and Dutch text:

            • Stock phrases such as “delve”, “plays a crucial role”, “in het huidige digitale landschap”.
            • Uniform sentence length. People write unevenly; models tend to keep a steady rhythm.
            • Stock transitions such as “Moreover”, “Furthermore”, “Bovendien”, “Daarnaast”.
            • Em dashes, used far more by models than by most writers.
            • “Not X, but Y” constructions and lists of three.
            • Uniform paragraphs and chatbot formatting (bold-label bullets, markdown headings).

            2. Deep check with Claude

            Sends the text to Claude, which judges how likely it is to be AI-written, quotes the passages that give it away, proposes rewrites in the document's own language and reviews clarity, structure, tone, concision and grammar.

            Limits

            No AI detector is reliable. Formal, templated or non-native writing can look machine-made, and edited AI text can look human. Use the result to improve a text, not as proof about who wrote it.

            Privacy

            History stays in this browser. The instant check never leaves your device. A deep check sends the text to the Anthropic API through this app's server, which does not store it.
