              How it works

              The checker looks at a document in two ways.

              1. Instant check

              Runs in your browser and scores eight signals that often show up in AI-generated English and Dutch text:

              • Stock phrases such as “delve”, “plays a crucial role”, “in het huidige digitale landschap”.
              • Uniform sentence length. People write unevenly; models tend to keep a steady rhythm.
              • Stock transitions such as “Moreover”, “Furthermore”, “Bovendien”, “Daarnaast”.
              • Em dashes, used far more by models than by most writers.
              • “Not X, but Y” constructions and lists of three.
              • Uniform paragraphs and chatbot formatting (bold-label bullets, markdown headings).

              2. Deep check with Claude

              Sends the text to Claude, which judges how likely it is to be AI-written, quotes the passages that give it away, proposes rewrites in the document's own language and reviews clarity, structure, tone, concision and grammar.

              3. Clean document

              Rewrites every paragraph that reads as machine-written, in the document's own language, while keeping every fact, name, number and legal wording. You get the result in the same format: a Word or PowerPoint file keeps its layout, styles, tables and speaker notes. The clean file also has:

              • no em dashes, stock phrases, formulaic transitions or chatbot formatting;
              • no hidden characters: zero-width characters, text-direction controls, hidden “tag” characters, variation selectors and unusual spaces are removed, and look-alike Cyrillic or Greek letters inside Latin words and fullwidth or “styled” letters are replaced with normal ones;
              • images keep their exact pixels but lose their metadata: EXIF, XMP, IPTC, C2PA content credentials and comments (EXIF stays when a photo needs it to display the right way up);
              • author and “last modified by” reset (or set to the name configured on the server), document comments field cleared, and custom properties that tag the file as AI-made removed. Sensitivity labels stay.

              After cleaning, the app lists every hidden trace it removed. Statistical watermarks hidden in word choice cannot be detected here; rewriting usually weakens them, but there is no guarantee they are gone, especially in paragraphs that were left unchanged.

              Inside a rewritten paragraph the formatting of its first word is applied to the whole paragraph, so check paragraphs that mixed bold or links before sharing the file.

              Limits

              No AI detector is reliable. Formal, templated or non-native writing can look machine-made, and edited AI text can look human. Use the result to improve a text, not as proof about who wrote it.

              Privacy

              History stays in this browser. The instant check never leaves your device. A deep check sends the text to the Anthropic API through this app's server, which does not store it.
